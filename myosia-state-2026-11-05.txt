Viewer (single self-contained world3d.html, raw WebGL1)
Camera-relative geometry-clipmap terrain (12 levels) + coarse far mesh; CPU mirror groundAt/hgtC must match GPU hgt(). Sky, shadows, shader water, instanced vegetation with regional habitat tables, floating Vortex islands (user: still has issues), rivers (inlaid beds), landforms/cliffs (unverified on real mountains).
Page needs: world3d-height/color/mask/biome/rivers.png, world3d-places.json. Units: 1 px = 15840 ft, FT = px per ft. The game layer lives inside start() (meta.places, cam, keys, groundAt, FT in scope); search for "var HERO" / "turn-based encounters" / "towns:" to find it.

Game layer
Walk mode: F walk/fly, V first/third person, WASD, Shift sprint, Space jump. Humanoid coloured by class/race of the active character (localStorage dnd.active, set by "play in 3D" in character.html).
E = encounter: region-based monsters as figures with name/HP/distance labels; click label to target. battle.html runs in a side-panel iframe (postMessage: dndState/dndPos/dndTarget/dndFoeMove/dndBattle). Movement budget = speed (Dash x2), melee reach 5 ft, ranged weapons use their own range (attack.rng [normal,long], e.g. thrown dagger 20/60), spells 120, opportunity attacks, Disengage, enemies move/dash. Walking locked outside your turn.
Towns: T near a place -> generated NPC, skill-check dialogue, "clear the roads" quest, J quest log (localStorage dnd.quests).
Levelling 1-5: dnd-levels.js + levelup.html. Engine extras in dnd-combat.js: Action Surge, sneak attack scaling, rage uses, Improved Critical, Disengage.
