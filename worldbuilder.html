Play online

One person is the Game Master and hosts the table. Everyone else joins as a player with the code. Players see only what the GM has revealed and can move only their own token. The free public relay is used, so keep it to casual play.

How it works

Move: drag a token to move it, drag empty space to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom. Hold Space and drag to pan from any tool.
Paint / Rectangle / Erase (GM): pick a terrain from the strip along the bottom, then draw. Ctrl+Z undoes.
Reveal / Hide (GM): turn on Fog of war in the Map tab, then paint what the players can see. Players get a black map outside the revealed area.
Ruler: drag to measure. Each square is 5 ft. Ping: click to flash a spot for everyone.
Tokens: add them in the Token tab. Hidden tokens and tokens standing in fog are never sent to players.
Map image: you can lay a Dungeon Scrawl or other PNG under the grid. Images are too big for the free relay, so each player loads the same file themselves and the GM's placement is shared.
Saving: maps save in this browser automatically. Use Export to send a map file to a friend.
